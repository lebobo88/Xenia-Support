## The path from inquiry to proposal

A project at Rob Lee Media starts with an inquiry: what the client needs, roughly when, where, and what the work is for.

Rob then has a discovery call with the client about the project — what the images or footage are for, the brand, the audience, and the constraints.

After that call, Rob sends a written proposal for that specific project.

## Why nothing is quoted before the call

Rob Lee Media prices and scopes each project individually, in that written proposal, because both depend on the scope of the work and on what the client will use it for. That is why no figure is given before the discovery call.

## What Rob needs to know about a project

The details Rob works from are the kind of shoot, the date or the window the client is working within, the location, the rough scope — how long, how many people or products, and what the finished work is for — and how to reach the client.
