## What this document is

This document records which facts about Rob Lee Media do not exist in published form. It exists so that a question about any of them has a grounded answer: the information is not published, rather than not found.

## Prices

Rob Lee Media publishes no prices, rates, packages, hourly or day rates, minimums, or deposit amounts. There is no rate card. Price comes from the written proposal that follows a discovery call, and it is specific to that project.

Market rates charged by other studios in Metro Detroit are not Rob Lee Media's prices and say nothing about them.

## Timelines

Rob Lee Media publishes no standard turnaround or delivery time. Delivery timelines are set per project in the project agreement, and depend on the scope of the shoot and the volume of the edit.

## Availability

Rob Lee Media publishes no calendar or availability. Whether a specific date is open is something only Rob can confirm.

## Terms

Rob Lee Media publishes no contract terms, licensing terms, or usage rights. How a client may use finished work — where, for how long, and in what form — is defined in that project's agreement and nowhere else.

## Delivery formats

Rob Lee Media publishes no standard file formats, resolutions, image counts, or delivery method. These are part of each project's agreement.

## Project status

The status of any individual project — what has been shot, what is being edited, when it will be delivered — is not recorded here. Only Rob has it.
