## Service area

Rob Lee Media is based in Metro Detroit, Michigan, and works within about a hundred miles of Detroit. That covers the Detroit metro area and much of southeast Michigan.

Rob Lee Media also shoots travel and documentary work, so locations outside that radius are handled case by case rather than as standard coverage.

## Local knowledge

Rob Lee Media works in and around Detroit and knows the area's locations and communities.
