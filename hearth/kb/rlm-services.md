## The studio

Rob Lee Media is a photography and cinematography studio in Metro Detroit, Michigan. Its work is described as story-driven: the images are made to carry the story behind a client's business, brand, or vision.

Rob Lee Media shoots both stills and motion.

## Events

Rob Lee Media covers events — corporate events and conferences, live performances, and product launches among them.

## Commercial

Rob Lee Media does commercial work: product photography, advertising and promotional campaigns, brand photography, and content for marketing and social media.

## Portraits

Rob Lee Media photographs people — professional headshots, personal branding sessions, lifestyle and creative portraits, and team or group photos.

## Fine art

Rob Lee Media makes fine art photography, including landscape, cityscape, nature, and architectural work.

## Travel and documentary

Rob Lee Media shoots travel and documentary work, including promotional content for destinations.

## Cinematography

Rob Lee Media shoots motion alongside stills, including brand films. What a video project involves is defined per project.
