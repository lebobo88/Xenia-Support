## Weddings

Rob Lee Media does not shoot weddings. This is a standing decision about the kind of work the studio takes, not a question of the date, the budget, or the size of the event. There is no wedding coverage of any kind and no partial wedding coverage, and Rob Lee Media does not keep a list of wedding photographers to refer people to.

Engagement portraits, couples portraits, and anniversary sessions asked for on their own — not as part of wedding coverage — are ordinary portrait work.

## Outside the listed categories

Work that falls outside events, commercial, portraits, fine art, travel and documentary, and cinematography is not something Rob Lee Media has a standing answer about. Rob decides case by case.
